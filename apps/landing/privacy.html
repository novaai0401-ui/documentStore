Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Pyntra ("we", "us") is a free studio for documents and design that runs entirely in your web browser at pyntra.tekivex.com. This policy explains what data is and isn't handled when you use it.

1. Your files stay on your device

Pyntra is built to be private by architecture. Opening, editing, converting, OCR, redaction, image and video processing, and design all happen locally in your browser. Your documents, photos and videos are never uploaded to our servers. Work you save is stored in your browser's local storage / IndexedDB on your own device so it can reopen automatically; you can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

2. Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate, anonymous usage (for example which tools are popular and which devices are used) so we can improve the product. Google Analytics sets cookies and may collect information such as a truncated IP address, device and browser type, and pages visited. It does not receive the contents of your files. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google Analytics Opt-out.

3. Advertising

We may display advertising served by Google, including through Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info/choices.

Advertising is never shown the contents of your documents; ads are served by Google based on cookies and context, not on your files.

4. Optional AI features

Some optional features send only the text you choose to a third-party AI provider so it can respond:

  • "Write with AI" (script writing). When you type a topic and tap the AI button, that topic is sent to our server, which forwards it to Groq's API to generate a script. Only the topic text you enter is sent — never your files. If no AI key is configured on the server, the button is hidden and nothing is sent.
  • Bring-your-own-key AI assistant. If you configure your own AI provider (e.g. OpenAI, Anthropic, Groq) in settings, the document text or question you ask is sent directly from your browser to the endpoint you configured, using your key. This is entirely under your control and off by default.

Please review the privacy policy of any AI provider you use. We do not store the prompts or responses on our servers.

5. Real-time collaboration

If you choose to share a design or document for real-time co-editing, edits are relayed between participants through a relay server. These frames are end-to-end encrypted — the relay only forwards already-encrypted data and cannot read your content. Collaboration is optional and only active when you create or join a share link.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use: (a) local storage / IndexedDB to autosave your work and remember preferences on your device; (b) analytics cookies (Section 2); and (c) advertising cookies (Section 3). You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; disabling them will not stop the core editing tools from working.

7. Children's privacy

Pyntra is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Your choices & rights

Because your files never leave your device, you remain in control of your own content. For analytics and advertising data handled by Google, you can use the opt-out links above. Depending on your location (e.g. the EEA, UK, or California) you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you; contact us to exercise them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact
