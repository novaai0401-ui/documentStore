What you can do with Pyntra

One private workspace for documents, design, images, video and PDF tools — all running locally in your browser. Click any tool in the editor to start; nothing is uploaded.

📄

Edit & convert PDFs

Open any PDF to fill forms, add text, highlight, sign, redact, rotate, reorder, merge and split pages — then download. Convert PDF ⇄ Word/images. Handles encrypted PDFs (RC4, AES-128, AES-256).

🗂️

Word, Excel & PowerPoint

Open and edit Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx) and OpenDocument files in their native formats — spreadsheets with formulas and charts, slide decks, and rich documents. Convert between them and PDF.

🎨

Design studio

Start from a blank canvas or a template: posters, invitations, business cards, coupons, social posts, quote cards and animated promos. Add text, shapes, images, your logo and brand colors.

🧑‍💼

Résumé & cover letter

Fill in your details and get a clean, editable résumé (sidebar, modern, minimal or elegant) plus a matching cover letter — export to PDF in a click.

🖼️

Images: convert & edit

Convert HEIC → JPG/PNG/WebP/AVIF/PDF, edit and crop images, remove backgrounds, build photo collages, and caption images with the meme maker.

🗜️

Compress & watermark

Shrink images and PDFs without visible quality loss, and stamp a watermark across PDFs and images in batch — privately, on your device.

📷

Scan & capture

Scan documents with your camera into a clean multi-page PDF (with OCR), record your screen or camera, and tidy the result in the video studio.

🎬

Video studio

Trim clips, turn video into a GIF, extract or add audio, and compress — a lightweight editor for quick edits, all in the browser.

🧾

PDF forms & QR

Fill existing PDF forms, or draw new fillable fields that Acrobat and Chrome recognize. Generate QR codes to embed in any design or document.

🔒

Protect & sign

Add or remove a PDF password, apply real digital signatures and stamps, and truly redact content so it’s gone from the file — not just hidden.

👥

Real-time collaboration

Share a link to co-edit a document or design across devices, with built-in chat, read receipts and screen sharing — end-to-end encrypted.

🌐

Private, offline, multilingual

Installable PWA that works offline, autosaves locally and reopens automatically. The interface is available in 17 languages. Nothing is ever uploaded.

How to use Pyntra

Three common flows. Each takes under a minute, and your files stay on your device the whole time.

📄

Edit a PDF

1. Open Pyntra and click Open document.
2. Choose your PDF — it loads locally.
3. Fill fields, add text, highlight, sign, redact or reorder pages.
4. Click Download. It autosaves so you can reopen it later.

🎨

Design something

1. Open Pyntra and pick Design something new or a template.
2. Edit text, swap colors, drop in images and your logo.
3. Use Magic Resize to reflow for other sizes.
4. Export as PNG or PDF, or animate it for social.

🧑‍💼

Build a résumé

1. Open Pyntra and choose Résumé builder.
2. Fill in your details and pick a style.
3. Add a matching cover letter if you need one.
4. Download the PDF — ready to send.

👥

Collaborate live

1. Open any document or design in Pyntra.
2. Click Share to get a secure link.
3. Send it — others edit with you in real time.
4. Chat or share your screen right in the room.

🖼️

Convert or compress

1. Open Pyntra and choose Convert images or Compress files.
2. Add your images or PDFs.
3. Pick the output format or size.
4. Download — done, with nothing uploaded.

🧾

Make a fillable PDF

1. Open Pyntra and choose PDF form builder.
2. Open your PDF and draw fields where you want them.
3. Name each field and pick text or checkbox.
4. Save — anyone can fill it in Acrobat or Chrome.

Frequently asked questions

What is Pyntra?

A free, private studio that runs entirely in your browser. Edit PDFs and Office documents, design, build résumés, work with images and video, create PDF forms, and collaborate — without uploading your files.

Is Pyntra free?

Yes — free to use, no account required, and no watermark on your output.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Everything runs client-side, so your documents stay on your device. Collaboration is end-to-end encrypted.

Can I edit Word, Excel & PowerPoint?

Yes — open and edit .docx, .xlsx, .pptx and OpenDocument in their native formats, and convert to/from PDF.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Install Pyntra as a PWA and it works offline; your work autosaves locally and reopens automatically.

Can I collaborate with others?

Yes — share a link to co-edit in real time across devices, with chat and screen sharing built in.
