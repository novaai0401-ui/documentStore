Ready-to-paste copy for X, Instagram, and YouTube, plus the thinking behind it.

## 0. The positioning (say this in one breath)

> **PDFCraft is a free, private PDF editor that runs entirely in your browser —
> edit text, fill forms, sign, OCR, redact and convert to Word/Excel, and your
> files never leave your device.**

Three hooks, ranked by how well they travel on social:

1. **Privacy** — "your files never get uploaded." This is the share-worthy one.
2. **Free / no Acrobat** — "do what Acrobat charges for, in a browser tab."
3. **Power features** — edit text like Word, AI assistant, OCR, true redaction.

Lead with #1 or #2 in the hook line; use #3 as proof.

## 5. Do these to actually get noticed (beyond one post)

- **Show, don't tell.** A 10-second screen recording of editing a PDF with no
  upload beats any static graphic. Make that your hero asset.
- **Post where the pain is.** Reddit r/productivity, r/software, r/pdf;
  Hacker News "Show HN"; relevant X/YouTube comment threads. Lead with the
  problem you solve, not the product.
- **One hook per post.** Don't list 15 features. Pick privacy OR free OR
  edit-like-Word and hammer it.
- **Consistency > virality.** 3 posts/week for a month, each a different hook,
  beats one big launch.
- **Make sharing free.** A clear free demo link with no sign-up wall is your
  best growth lever — keep it one click away in every bio/description.
